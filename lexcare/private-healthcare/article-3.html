Concierge Medicine: The Premium Care Model Reshaping Patient Access

LM
Dr. Lisa Morgan|Jun 22, 2026

Concierge medicine is experiencing rapid growth as patients seek more personalized healthcare experiences. The model involves annual retainer fees of $1,500 to $25,000 in exchange for enhanced physician access, longer appointments, and comprehensive preventive care. An estimated 8,200 concierge practices operate in the US in 2026, a 34% increase from 2023.

The model addresses pain points in traditional healthcare. In conventional practices, physicians see 20-30 patients per day with 12-15 minute appointments. Concierge practices limit panels to 400-600 patients, enabling 30-60 minute appointments and same-day access.

The legal framework for concierge medicine varies by state. In most states, concierge practices are permitted provided the retainer fee is structured as a service fee rather than insurance. However, the intersection of concierge fees and insurance coverage requires careful legal navigation.

KEY INSIGHT: Concierge medicine is expanding beyond primary care into specialties including cardiology and dermatology. The model's emphasis on physician autonomy and patient access is attracting both patients and physicians.

The growth of concierge medicine is reshaping the competitive landscape. Traditional practices are responding with hybrid models, and healthcare systems are launching concierge programs to attract high-net-worth patients. As the market matures, the model will continue evolving with new service offerings and technology integration.
