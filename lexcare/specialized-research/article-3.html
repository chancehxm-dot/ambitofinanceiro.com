AI Diagnostics and Liability: Who Bears Responsibility When Algorithms Err?

TL
Dr. Thomas Lee, MD, JD|Jun 25, 2026

The integration of AI into medical diagnostics creates a fundamental challenge for legal liability frameworks. When an AI system contributes to diagnostic error, determining responsibility becomes a complex legal determination that existing law is ill-equipped to answer.

The current framework operates across three legal theories: medical malpractice, product liability, and negligence. The interplay creates a complex landscape where multiple parties may bear responsibility.

Court decisions reveal a trend toward shared responsibility. Physicians were held solely liable in 44% of cases, vendors in 22%, and shared liability was imposed in 19%.

KEY INSIGHT: The liability framework is evolving toward shared responsibility. Healthcare organizations must implement comprehensive AI governance frameworks including validation protocols and clinical oversight procedures.

The FDA's predetermined change control plan framework and the EU's AI Act establish requirements that may inform liability determinations. Healthcare organizations deploying AI tools should implement governance frameworks including pre-deployment validation, clinical oversight procedures, and staff training.
