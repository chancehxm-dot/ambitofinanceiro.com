About Market Terminal

Terminal-style financial intelligence for modern investors

Our Mission

Market Terminal was founded with a clear mission: to bring institutional-grade financial data and analysis to every investor through a terminal-style interface that is both powerful and accessible. In an era where information moves at the speed of light, we believe that sophisticated market intelligence should not be the exclusive domain of Wall Street trading desks and hedge fund research departments. Our platform aggregates, analyzes, and presents complex financial data in a clean, terminal-inspired dashboard format that empowers individual investors, financial advisors, and institutional clients alike.

What We Do

We operate at the intersection of financial markets, data science, and technology. Our team of quantitative analysts, economists, and software engineers processes millions of data points daily — from tick-level futures pricing and stock performance metrics to fund flow analysis and technology sector trends. We transform this raw information into actionable intelligence through real-time dashboards, in-depth research reports, and proprietary analytical frameworks. Our coverage spans four core domains: Futures Trading, US Stocks, Fund Investing, and Tech Finance.

Our Editorial Standards

Every piece of analysis published on Market Terminal undergoes a rigorous review process. Our research is grounded in verifiable data, transparent methodology, and academic rigor. We cite our sources, disclose our assumptions, and clearly distinguish between objective data analysis and subjective interpretation. Our writers and analysts bring decades of combined experience from institutions including Goldman Sachs, BlackRock, JPMorgan Chase, and leading fintech companies. Each team member is committed to intellectual honesty and analytical excellence.

Part of the FinancePulse Network

Market Terminal is a proud member of the FinancePulse network, a family of financial media properties dedicated to serving the global investment community. Alongside FinancePulse — our flagship news platform covering Insurance, Legal Services, SaaS & IT, Personal Finance, and Online Education — and Finance Journal — our data-driven market intelligence publication — Market Terminal completes a comprehensive ecosystem of financial information services. Each property maintains its own editorial independence while sharing a common commitment to quality, accuracy, and reader service.
